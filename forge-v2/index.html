HOW IT WORKS

01
DESCRIBE YOUR WORKOUT
Type something like "10 rounds 40 seconds work 20 seconds rest" or say it using the microphone. FORGE understands natural language in English and Spanish.
02
REVIEW AND START
FORGE instantly creates the timer structure. Hit Start — a 3-2-1 countdown prepares you. Then the timer runs automatically through every phase.

The screen changes color for each phase. Voice cues guide you. Beeps signal transitions. You just focus on your workout.
